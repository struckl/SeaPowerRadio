1.4.2: aliases.txt can keep a callsign.
Fixed: a line like "Akira O-ren = Victory" in BepInEx/config/SeaPowerRadio/aliases.txt was refused, because Victory is one of the mod's own callsigns, not a ship name. Now any ship, submarine or shore callsign word works there (Bulwark, Sentinel, Victory, Warden, Guardian, Valiant, Resolute, Steadfast, Vigilant, Defiant, Intrepid, Courage, Shadow, Silent, Deepwater, Barracuda, Castle, Bastion, Harbor, Lighthouse). The ship keeps it in every mission and no other unit gets it.
Not run in the game yet; checked offline for the globe beta and 0.8.7.
