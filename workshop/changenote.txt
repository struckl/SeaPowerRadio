1.4.0: voices follow the nation, a new random mix each mission, fuel calls for the new game build.
Ship names and squadron words are spoken in the accent family of their country. New setting "Random voices each mission" (on by default): every fresh mission start mixes the voices anew, ships included; loading a save keeps that mission's voices. Female voices are off by default now (a config saved with the old default is switched once; turn them back on and they stay on).
The fuel calls (bingo and the like) read the new fuel model of the globe public beta (build 261107) and keep the old one for older builds.
Run in the game on the globe beta 261107: calls play in a mission and the fuel calls were watched; the new voice choices are not run in the game yet. 0.8.7 checked offline only.
