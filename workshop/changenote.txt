1.4.1: a voice gender per squadron.
New: a text file, BepInEx/config/SeaPowerRadio/squadrons.txt, with lines like "Bear Aces = female" or "Ghostriders = male" gives every pilot of that squadron a voice of that gender, whatever the Female voices setting says. There is no menu entry; the file is created with examples at the first mission and applies from the next mission.
Not run in the game yet; checked offline for the globe beta and 0.8.7.
