1.2.0: voice packs.
Every country can now have its own voice pack, set in Options > Audio > Rats Radio > Voice packs (NATO, Warsaw Pact and Other groups, one drop-down per country). The 43 voices are the NATO (English) pack; more languages can follow as separate Workshop items.
A country without a pack keeps the game's own voice. If you play a side other than the US, UK, Canada, Australia or New Zealand, you now hear the game's voice by default instead of the English radio voices. Pick NATO (English) for that country to get the old behaviour. The choice applies from the next mission.
