# Panic recording script (option 1: performed panic, converted into the 43 voices)

Why: the voice models cannot invent real fear; a person can. One performer acts the lines once, and a voice converter
(Chatterbox's own converter, Seed-VC as the second try) moves the performance into each R.A.T.S. Radio voice, keeping
the panic. Background: `_render-handover/panic-options.md` (option 1).

## Who

- **First test:** anyone, even a phone recording of yourself: 5 lines (marked **T**) are enough to hear whether the
  conversion keeps the panic.
- **Full set later:** one man and one woman (female voices need a female performer). The performer's accent shows
  through a little: a neutral English or American accent fits most voices; a native German accent would leak into all of
  them.
- Every performer gives one written line (Discord message is fine):
  *"I release my recordings for R.A.T.S. Radio under CC0 (public domain); they may be changed and shared freely."*
  Without it the take cannot ship.

## How to record

- Phone or headset, quiet room, no music. Hold the phone about an arm's length away: shouting into it from close
  clips the recording, and the mod adds its own overdrive later.
- Warm up first: 30 seconds of jumping jacks or push-ups, then record straight away. Out of breath is the point.
- **Do not act "radio".** Act the moment: you are hit, you are burning, the water is coming in. Breaking, repeating,
  running out of air, swallowing words and unfinished lines are all good. Do not read clean.
- Three takes per line, a breath between them. One file per line is easiest; one long file with all takes is fine too.
- Names: `p01.wav`, `p02.wav` ... (any format: wav, m4a, mp3). Note the performer's name and man/woman.
- No numbers or bearings: the mod adds those from its own clips.

## What happens with the takes

1. The **T** lines go through the test first (on the main PC, under an hour): converted into three voices (US man,
   Scottish man, one woman) with both converters, through the radio filter, played next to today's stressed take.
2. If it works: the full set, converted into all 43 voices, becomes the **panic** mood (with the radio effects of
   option 2 on top), shipped like the other moods.
3. Credits: the performers by name (or as they wish) next to the existing voice credits.
