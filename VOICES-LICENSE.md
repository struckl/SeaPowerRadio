# Sea Power Radio – voice library license and credits

The rendered voice clips of Sea Power Radio (`voices/<persona>/*.mp3`, shipped with the mod) are licensed under the
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0)**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

They were generated with Chatterbox (Resemble AI, MIT license), each of the 43 voices cloned from one speaker of the
CSTR VCTK Corpus, and put through a radio filter. The clips therefore derive from:

> Yamagishi, Junichi; Veaux, Christophe; MacDonald, Kirsten (2019). CSTR VCTK Corpus: English Multi-speaker Corpus
> for CSTR Voice Cloning Toolkit (version 0.92). University of Edinburgh. The Centre for Speech Technology Research
> (CSTR). https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2645 – licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Changes made: speech synthesized from new text in the speakers' cloned voices, cut into phrases, filtered to sound
like military radio. The audio carries Resemble AI's imperceptible Perth watermark.

When you share or adapt the clips, credit "Sea Power Radio (github.com/struckl/SeaPowerRadio), voices derived from
the CSTR VCTK Corpus (University of Edinburgh, CC BY 4.0)" and link the license.

The plugin code and the voice pipeline are under the MIT license (`LICENSE`).
